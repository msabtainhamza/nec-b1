| ADR-022 | 2026-09-28 | Product owner decision: where the PRD leaves a business rule open, default to SAP Business One conventions as understood from general knowledge. (a) R-01: cancelling an erroneous payment also cancels its remaining reconciliations with invoices in one atomic step, as B1 does, so payment reversal is exempt from the REV-05 allocation block; invoice reversal still requires unallocation first. (b) R-02: A/P invoices belong to Purchasing (M3); incoming and outgoing payments belong to Banking (M4). (c) R-03: service lines and non-inventory items may be invoiced directly from a sales order without a delivery. For stock items, V1 keeps the PRD rule (invoice from delivery) rather than B1's option of an invoice that issues stock itself. (d) R-04: the first document that moves stock owns the inventory and cost effect. A goods receipt posts inventory against an allocation (goods received not invoiced) account; a supplier invoice based on a receipt clears allocation to payable, with price differences to inventory or a price-difference account; a delivery posts cost of goods sold against inventory; a customer invoice posts receivable, revenue and tax. (e) R-11: payment means (cash, bank transfer, cheque) map to G/L accounts; payments may remain partly or wholly on account (unreconciled) and appear in partner balances and aging. (f) R-15: one invoice may be copied from several deliveries or receipts of the same partner (N:1). (g) R-06: documents use open, closed and cancelled states plus drafts; approval uses drafts pending approval. (h) Business partners follow B1 master data: code, type customer/supplier/lead, group, contact persons, bill-to and ship-to addresses, payment terms, credit limit, active/inactive; no hard delete. | Lets implementation proceed without waiting for each open question. Supersedes nothing: ADR-007 (one base currency, no foreign-currency posting) and ADR-009 (local accountant and tax sign-off) still apply; B1 behavior does not establish local compliance. Items (a)-(g) amend how REV-05, SAL-04, FIN-02 and section 20 are read and must be confirmed by the product owner and an accountant before posting features ship. B1 behavior is recalled from general knowledge and may differ in detail from the actual product. | Provisional; verification and accountant sign-off pending |
| ADR-023 | 2026-09-28 | Inventory master data follows Business One structure with V1 simplifications: each item has one base unit of measure with a configured decimal precision (no purchase or sales unit conversions yet; review R-07); item types are inventory, non-inventory and service; item groups may override inventory, COGS and revenue accounts, otherwise G/L determination applies; each warehouse belongs to one branch, and a branch's first warehouse becomes its default; price lists are sales or purchase lists in the company currency, with optional validity dates and one default per purpose. | Keeps stock quantities unambiguous before the stock ledger exists. Unit conversions, per-warehouse valuation and batch/serial tracking remain later work. The reservation policy in R-07 is still open and will be decided with sales orders. | Implemented; product-owner confirmation pending |
| ADR-024 | 2026-09-28 | Purchasing and stock ledger rules for V1: (a) weighted-average (moving average) cost per item across the company, not per warehouse; (b) line amounts use half-up rounding to 4 decimals (net price = price x (100 - discount%) / 100, line total = quantity x net price), average cost is stored to 6 decimals; (c) a goods receipt posts Dr inventory (warehouse account, else item-group account, else G/L determination) and Cr Goods Received Not Invoiced at the order net price; non-inventory and service lines create no stock movement and no journal at receipt (their expense is recognised on the A/P invoice); (d) receipts cannot exceed open order quantity (no tolerance yet); a fully received order closes automatically; (e) a receipt is corrected only by a full cancellation document in the same series, dated on or after the receipt in an open period, blocked while supplier invoices use it, and blocked with VALUATION_BLOCKED unless each item's latest stock movement is still this receipt, so no retrospective recosting is ever needed; cancellation reopens an order closed by full receipt but not one closed manually; (f) stock can never go negative (database constraints); item type and unit lock once an item appears on a document. | Implements PUR-01, PUR-02, INV-01, INV-06, REV-03 to REV-05 and the goods-receipt row of section 6.2 for this slice, and resolves the costing-scope part of R-04. The per-item valuation scope, the non-stock receipt treatment and the strict latest-movement reversal rule need accountant confirmation with worked examples; tax is not calculated yet. | Implemented; accountant sign-off pending |
| ADR-025 | 2026-09-28 | A/P invoice rules for V1: (a) an A/P invoice is copied from one or more posted goods receipts of the same vendor (N:1), up to each receipt line's uninvoiced quantity, dated on or after the receipts in an open period; direct invoices without a receipt are not supported yet; (b) journal: Dr Goods Received Not Invoiced at the receipt value for stocked lines, the difference between invoice and receipt value to the Price Difference account (debit or credit; stock is not revalued), Dr Purchased Services and Supplies (new account 5400, determination key purchase_expense) for non-stocked lines, and Cr the vendor through the payables control account; (c) three-way match: quantity can never exceed the received quantity; a unit price outside the company tolerance (purchasing settings, default 0%) is rejected unless the user holds pur.invoice.override and sets the override, which is audited; (d) a vendor invoice number can be used only once per vendor among posted invoices (case-insensitive); (e) due date defaults to document date plus vendor payment-term days; (f) a full cancellation document reverses the journal and restores receipt invoiced quantities, only when no payment is applied, once per invoice; an invoiced receipt cannot be cancelled until its invoices are cancelled. | Implements PUR-03, FIN-04 partner balance, and the invoice row of section 6.2 without payments. Sending price differences to an expense account rather than revaluing stock keeps receipt reversals safe but differs from Business One's default for items still in stock; the accountant must confirm this, and whether invoice cancellation is a legally valid correction in the target jurisdiction (REV-01). Tax is still not calculated. | Partially superseded by ADR-026 (items a, b and tax); c-f remain in force |
| ADR-026 | 2026-09-28 | Product owner decision: align the ADR-025 open points with SAP Business One behaviour, as understood from general knowledge. (a) Price differences on stocked receipt lines revalue stock still on hand, split proportionally (quantity on hand divided by invoiced quantity); only the remainder goes to the Price Difference account; revaluation is a value-only stock movement, and invoice cancellation reverses it under the same latest-movement valuation check (VALUATION_BLOCKED otherwise). (b) A/P invoices without a goods receipt are allowed: item-type invoices take item lines (stocked items are received into a warehouse by the invoice itself; non-stocked items post to purchase expense) alongside receipt-based lines; service-type invoices take G/L account lines only (active, postable, non-control accounts). (c) Tax: tenant-defined tax codes (purchase, sales or both) with dated, append-only rates; the rate valid on the posting date is snapshotted on each line; line tax = line total x rate, half-up to 4 decimals; input tax posts to the input tax account and is included in the vendor payable. Only a 0% No tax code is seeded; no statutory rates are supplied. (d) Invoice cancellation remains the correction mechanism (as in Business One); A/P credit memos are later work. (e) The price tolerance is editable in Administration > System Initialization > Document Settings. | Supersedes ADR-025 (a) and (b) and its no-tax limitation. Business One behaviour is recalled from general knowledge and may differ in detail. Tax codes do not constitute jurisdictional compliance; ADR-009 still requires local accountant and tax sign-off before production. | Implemented; accountant and jurisdiction sign-off pending |
